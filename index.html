about

I write — in Arabic, English, and French — and read political essays for fun, usually with a terminal open in the other window. Between that, I code, edit video, design, and put together content for social media. Most of what I build starts from the same instinct: strip away what isn't needed, keep what actually helps.

On the desktop, that means switching between bspwm, dwm, and KDE Plasma depending on the mood, running patched, minimal builds of the suckless suite. On the creative side, it means replacing bloated software with lighter tools I've built myself when I can.

I daily-drive Debian, partly because I love it, partly because (unlike some distro-hoppers out there) I'm mentally stable and feel secure. 😄
